About

I'm Mohammad. I've been writing software since 2015.

Full-stack engineer based in Cairo. I spend most of my time in the .NET ecosystem, but I work across C, C++, Rust, TypeScript, and Dart when a project needs it. I care about clean architecture, documented decisions, and shipping real things real people use.

The long version

I've been building software since I was ten years old.

It started with games. Unreal Engine blueprints, then Unity, then a slow obsession with how computers actually work underneath. That path took me through graphics, assembly, OS development, and one accidental rebuild of ASP.NET from scratch before I discovered it already existed.

Today I ship full-stack software for clients and product teams, and I publish open-source .NET libraries on NuGet.

How I work

What a project looks like when I'm on it.

The style of working with me, in four points.

Interfaces before implementations

Every system I build starts with the shape of the interfaces. Replaceability is a feature, not a nice-to-have.

Decisions written down

Every architectural choice ships with its decision record. Alternatives, tradeoffs, consequences. The reasoning is the product.

Weekly progress you can see

Working software in your hands every week. If something is wrong you find out in week two, not week twelve.

Own the outcome

Once I'm on a project, I'm accountable for what ships. Not just the code I wrote. The full deliverable.

What I don't do

The honest section.

Everyone overpromises. Here's what I'll turn down or route elsewhere, so you don't waste a call finding out.

Firmware & embedded hardware design

I understand the boundary, can write firmware when it's needed, and know my way around KiCad and an Arduino. But it's not my sharpest tool. On projects that need real embedded engineering, I'll bring in a specialist.

ML model training & data science

I can integrate ML models into a system. I don't train them. If your project is centered on ML research, I'm not the right hire.

Graphic design & brand identity

I build clean, functional UIs. I don't create brand systems, logos, or marketing visuals. For those, bring in a designer.

Business viability consulting

I'll tell you whether your idea is technically feasible. I won't tell you whether it's a good business. That's a different conversation with a different expert.

When a project crosses into one of these areas, I say so early. If it makes sense to bring in a specialist, I bring one in and run the delivery. You still get one point of contact and one accountable owner.
