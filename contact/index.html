FAQ

Before you write.

If yours isn't here, send a message. I answer every one.

Ask a question
What's your rate? + −

Pricing is on inquiry. Every engagement is quoted individually after the first conversation. The services page explains the engagement models, and the written proposal covers scope, timeline, and price.

Do you sign NDAs? + −

Yes, standard mutual NDAs on request.

Are you available for full-time roles? + −

Yes, for the right team. The embedded engagement model covers this. Mention it in your message and I'll treat it accordingly.

What's your time zone? + −

Egypt uses EET (UTC+2) in winter and EEST (UTC+3) during daylight saving. The current offset and local time are shown with the contact details.

Can you travel for on-site work? + −

Yes, when it matters. Most of my work is remote, but I'll travel for kickoff, on-site integration, or critical milestones if the project needs it.

How soon can you start? + −

Depends on current commitments. I'll tell you honestly in the reply. Usually within two to four weeks for a new project, sooner for something small.

If it's not a fit, I'll say so.

I'd rather turn down a project than take one I can't do well. If your project isn't in my wheelhouse, I'll either bring in someone better or point you to someone who can. Either way, you'll hear back within one business day.
