Process

How an engagement goes.

01

Intro call — 30 minutes

You describe the problem, I ask questions. By the end I'll tell you yes, no, or here's someone better for this. No pitch, no commitment.

02

Written proposal — 3–5 days

Scope, deliverables, timeline, price, and the assumptions the project rests on. Written down so we both know what we're agreeing to.

03

Kickoff & architecture — 1–3 weeks

The first real deliverable is a design, not code. Interface layout, data model, integration points, decision records. You see the shape of the system before the first sprint starts.

04

Iterative delivery

Working software in your hands every week. Review and adjust every two. If something is wrong, you find out in week two, not week twelve.

05

Handoff

Repository, READMEs, decision records, deployment notes, a walkthrough recording. Everything needed for someone else to pick it up. I'm not trying to lock you in.

06

Post-launch — optional

A retainer or on-call arrangement if you want me to keep shipping. Otherwise the project is done and you own it. No lock-in, no surprise invoices.

FAQ

Questions worth answering up front.

What's your exact rate? + −

On inquiry. The ranges on the pricing cards are indicative, but every engagement is quoted individually after the intro call. Guessing in advance is how both sides end up unhappy.

Do you work with non-technical founders? + −

Frequently. Most of my clients come in with an idea, not a spec. My job in the first phase is to translate the idea into something we can both agree is worth building.

What if my project needs something you don't do? + −

I'll tell you. Firmware, embedded hardware design, ML model training. These aren't my sharpest tools. On projects that need them, I bring in a specialist I trust and run the delivery. You still get one point of contact and one accountable owner.

Who owns the code? + −

You do. All source code, all documentation, all decision records. Transferred on final delivery. No lock-in.

Can I host it myself, or do I need you to host it? + −

Either works. The code is yours to deploy wherever you want. If you'd rather not handle operations, I can host and maintain it for a monthly fee — but it's optional, not required.

Can you work with my existing team? + −

Yes. Embedded engagements are for exactly this. I join, hold the architecture, ship alongside your engineers, and hand off cleanly when the work is done.

Do you sign NDAs? + −

Yes, standard mutual NDAs on request. I don't require them for most projects.

What's your typical timeline? + −

Focused builds run 2–6 weeks. Full projects 2–6 months. Retainers and embedded engagements are open-ended. Rush timelines are available at higher cost.

What if the scope changes mid-project? + −

It usually does, and that's normal. When it does, I write an updated proposal with the new scope, timeline, and price. Nothing proceeds until you approve it.

Do you take on full-time roles? + −

Yes, for the right team. The embedded engagement model covers this. If you're hiring and want to explore it, use the contact page.
